HOW IT WORKS

Simple from the first conversation.

You shouldn't have to wonder what happens after you send an enquiry. We keep the process clear, practical and focused on getting the right solution in place.

THE JOURNEY

From idea to finished work.

Every project is different, but the basic journey stays simple. We first understand what you're trying to achieve, then work out the details, agree on the scope and move into production or development.

For larger or more involved projects, we'll keep you updated along the way.

01

Tell us what you need

Send us your idea, requirements, quantities, content, references or project details. You don't need to have everything figured out before contacting us.

02

We understand the brief

We'll ask the important questions, clarify specifications and identify anything that needs to be supplied before we can proceed.

03

We recommend a solution

Based on the project, we'll work out the most practical route — including materials, format, design, printing, development or other requirements.

04

Quote & approval

You'll receive the relevant pricing and project details. Work only moves forward once the scope and quotation have been approved.

05

Production or development

We create, print, develop or prepare the agreed work while keeping the project aligned with the approved requirements.

06

Review where needed

For projects that require client input, we'll give you the opportunity to review the relevant proof, draft or development stage before finalisation.

07

Delivery or launch

Once everything is ready, your finished product is delivered or your digital project is prepared to go live.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Clear communication. No unnecessary runaround.

We want clients to know what they're paying for, what we need from them and what happens next. If something changes during a project, we'll communicate it rather than leaving you guessing.
