# Animo Apparel Pricing — Internal

## Base cost
- Estimated landed cost: R150 per shirt
- Assumption: shirt + front print, with client-supplied artwork

## Current starting rates
| Quantity | Selling price | Gross profit per shirt |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–5 | R250 | R100 |
| 6–10 | R235 | R85 |
| 11–20 | R220 | R70 |
| 21–50 | R205 | R55 |
| 51+ | Quote | Quote |

## Design fees
- Simple artwork/edit: from R75
- Custom design: from R150
- Complex artwork: quote separately

## Add-ons to price separately
- Back print
- Sleeve print
- Names/numbers
- Alternative garments
- Delivery
- Rush orders

## Pricing rules
1. Base shirt price assumes front print only and client-supplied artwork.
2. Do not discount below the approved quantity tiers without checking actual costs and margin.
3. Quote unusual garment types and multi-location printing separately.
4. Review supplier costs regularly; R150 is an estimate, not a permanent cost.
5. Keep the detailed quantity tiers internal unless there is a marketing reason to publish them.
