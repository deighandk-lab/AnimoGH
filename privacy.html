Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Animo Group Holdings operates Animo Printing and Animo Digital Solutions in Benoni, Gauteng, South Africa. This notice explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website and services.

Information we collect

When you enquire, we receive your name, phone number, optional email address, service selection and project details. Website providers may process IP addresses, device and browser information, usage data and cookies. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information or information about others unless necessary and authorised.

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, communicate about projects, deliver agreed services, maintain website security and meet applicable legal obligations. We use information necessary to take steps requested by you or perform an agreement, comply with law, pursue legitimate business interests where appropriate, or rely on consent where required. Marketing is subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

Third-party services and transfers

Cloudflare hosts and secures this website. FormSubmit processes website enquiries for delivery to our email inbox. Google Analytics measures website usage; Google Fonts supplies fonts. Email and WhatsApp are available as contact channels. These providers may process information outside South Africa under their own terms and privacy notices. We are responsible for ensuring appropriate safeguards where POPIA requires them.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions. You can manage cookies through browser settings and use Google's analytics opt-out tools. Blocking cookies may affect some website functions.

Retention and security

We aim to retain information only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, contractual requirements, dispute resolution or applicable law. We aim to use reasonable safeguards against unauthorised access, loss and misuse. No online transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to or correction of your information, request deletion where applicable, object to certain processing and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email animogroupholdings@gmail.com or contact us on 068 714 3564. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our services or information practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
